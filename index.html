gitback2life is a real-world rebuild powered by curiosity, AI, code, and persistence. The mission is simple: turn a long interruption into a new beginning—one skill, one project, and one step toward independence at a time.

First milestone One month of continued access to the AI tools being used to learn and build.

The story

The person behind gitback2life has been disabled for approximately 14 years and is now working toward a more normal, independent life. The project is pseudonymous for privacy, but the public story is intended to remain truthful.

Rebuild

Establish routines, solve practical problems, and regain momentum.

Learn

Use AI to learn modern skills, especially programming and technology.

Independence

Turn skills and projects into legitimate opportunities and sustainable self-support.

Direct, specific support

Help keep the tools available

Rather than asking supporters to send money directly, the preferred mechanism is a legitimate OpenAI gift card or another official ChatGPT support method when eligible. Support is voluntary and there is no promised financial return.

OpenAI gift-card information

Never post a gift-card PIN, claim link, password, or other private information publicly. Eligibility and rules can change, so official OpenAI instructions should be checked before any transaction.

Privacy promise

gitback2life is pseudonymous by design. The project does not publicly publish a legal name, home address, personal phone number, medical documentation, government identification, banking details, or other identifying information.
