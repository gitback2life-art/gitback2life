# First Support Milestone — Current Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-08

## Question

What is the safest and most practical way to request support for continued access to the AI tools being used to build gitback2life?

## Current official OpenAI options

### OpenAI ChatGPT gift cards

OpenAI's current Help Center says ChatGPT gift cards are available in the United States through participating authorized retailers. They can be sent to friends and family and redeemed on ChatGPT web. The balance can be used for eligible ChatGPT subscriptions, renewals, upgrades, and certain credit purchases. Gift cards are available for use with ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, and Pro accounts, subject to account eligibility and U.S./USD requirements.

Important: a gift-card PIN is confidential and must never be posted publicly.

Source:
https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001491-buying-and-redeeming-openai-gift-cards

### Gifting ChatGPT credits

OpenAI also currently documents a separate gifting-credits feature that is rolling out gradually.

Eligible Plus and Pro personal accounts can purchase gifts. A recipient can redeem a gifted-credit claim on an eligible personal Free, Go, Plus, or Pro account. Claim links should be treated like passwords and shared privately.

This is a separate mechanism from gift cards and may not be available to every purchaser yet.

Source:
https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001417

## Possible community route: r/Assistance

The current r/Assistance page says it supports short-term assistance requests. Its listed basics include:

- at least 400 comment karma to make a request;
- regular Reddit activity, particularly during the previous 60 days;
- registration before making a request;
- no loans, referrals, illegal requests, or violations of terms;
- no personal payment information such as Cash App tags or PayPal emails in the public post.

Source:
https://www.reddit.com/r/Assistance/

## Implication for gitback2life

The project should not publish a support request there until the account is actually eligible under the community's current rules.

Even once eligible, the request should remain narrow and transparent:

**Purpose:** maintain access to the AI tools being used for learning and software development.

**Requested support:** direct OpenAI gift card or official ChatGPT gifted credits, where the giver independently chooses to help.

**Privacy:** no public payment identifiers, gift-card PINs, claim links, or unnecessary personal information.

**Framing:** voluntary support as a bridge while building skills and legitimate independence—not a promise of financial return.

## Current recommendation

**Do not post the support request yet.**

The official OpenAI support mechanisms are currently available, but the most obvious community route has eligibility requirements that should be met before posting.

The project should continue building a genuine public record first. When a suitable community route is eligible and useful, the prepared support request can be finalized against the community's then-current rules.
