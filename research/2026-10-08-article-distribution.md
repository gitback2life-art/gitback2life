# First Audience Development — Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-08

## Research question
How can gitback2life begin reaching its first readers when there is no existing audience or personal distribution network?

## Why it matters
The first Hashnode article has been published, but the project is new. Directly sharing the link with friends or followers is not a usable next step until such a network exists. The immediate goal is to create genuine discoverability and earn initial readers through useful participation and concrete lessons.

## Sources
- Hashnode Code of Conduct (official; effective March 7, 2026, last updated June 4, 2026): https://hashnode.com/code-of-conduct
- DEV Community Terms and Content Policy (official; checked 2026-10-08): https://dev.to/terms
- DEV Community Code of Conduct (official): https://dev.to/code-of-conduct
- Recent discussion analyzing rules across developer subreddits (published September 27, 2026): https://www.reddit.com/r/SoloDevelopment/comments/1wrnl94/where_a_solo_dev_can_actually_post_an_app_or_game/
- Recent r/SideProject discussion on promotion fatigue (June 8, 2026): https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1u07rgx/the_selfish_promotion_epidemic_is_killing_this_sub/

## Findings
1. Hashnode encourages relevant technical posts, questions, discussions, and links, but prohibits spam, bulk posting, engagement farming, misleading links, and using the platform primarily for self-promotion. AI-assisted work must be reviewed by the author.
2. DEV Community's official terms say posts should be on-topic and substantial, not designed primarily for promotion/backlinks, and may not merely point to an external link containing the full post. This makes a useful native article a better fit than a link-drop.
3. A recent review of developer subreddit rules reports that many large programming communities restrict project promotion or allow it only in dedicated threads. This is a secondary source, not a substitute for each community's live rules.
4. Reddit's live r/SideProject rules page could not be retrieved during this check. Recent community discussion also shows frustration with people who appear only to promote their own work. Treat r/SideProject as a candidate to verify later, not as cleared for immediate posting.
5. There is no established audience to message directly. The earlier suggestion to send the article to interested contacts was premature and is not the current plan.

## Evaluation
- **Start where an account already exists:** Use Hashnode for genuine participation and a useful follow-up post grounded in a real project event.
- **Make the next post valuable on its own:** A strong candidate is the concrete lesson that a feature believed complete was found broken when the actual source was inspected, followed by the cause, fix, and evidence boundary. This is specific, honest, and useful to other beginner builders; it should not be merely a link to the existing story.
- **Consider DEV.to later:** Its policy supports original, substantial content but rejects posts primarily intended for promotion or backlinks. Consider a genuinely useful adapted article only if it adds value and the operator wants to create/use that presence.
- **Use Reddit as a later test, not the first move:** First identify a specific relevant community, read its live rules, inspect recent accepted posts, and participate helpfully. Do not post the same promotion across multiple communities.
- **Measure early progress realistically:** Track posts published, relevant discussions joined, useful replies received, reader questions, and lessons learned. Do not invent reach, followers, or success; silence is not enough evidence to declare failure.

## Recommendation
Build the first audience from zero by publishing useful work and participating where the target readers already are. The next practical step is to draft a concrete, evidence-based Hashnode follow-up post from an already documented debugging lesson, then review it for accuracy and privacy before publishing. No new platform account or community posting venue is adopted by this recommendation.

## Uncertainties
- The live r/SideProject rules were not available to verify.
- We do not yet know which channel will produce meaningful readers for this particular project.
- No additional article, community post, or promotion has been published as part of this research.

## Decision
Research and recommendation only. No new platform or posting venue has been adopted by the operator.
