# First Article Distribution — Research

**Research date:** 2026-10-08

## Research question
How should gitback2life give its first Hashnode article a small, respectful distribution test without spamming communities or creating unnecessary accounts?

## Why it matters
The latest progress entry says to share the article thoughtfully with a few relevant people or communities, then learn from real responses. This should be a small experiment, not a broad marketing campaign.

## Sources
- Hashnode Code of Conduct (official; last updated June 4, 2026): https://hashnode.com/code-of-conduct
- Hashnode Terms (official): https://hashnode.com/terms
- Reddit search result from r/SoloDevelopment discussing subreddit promotion rules reviewed September 24–26, 2026: https://www.reddit.com/r/SoloDevelopment/comments/1wrnl94/where_a_solo_dev_can_actually_post_an_app_or_game/
- Reddit discussion of r/SideProject posting and removal friction (January 21, 2026): https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1qike30/what_are_the_nonobvious_rules_for_this_subreddit/

## Findings
1. Hashnode's official Code of Conduct welcomes relevant technical sharing and links, but prohibits spam, bulk posting, engagement farming, and using the platform primarily for self-promotion. AI-assisted content remains the author's responsibility.
2. Recent community-rule research suggests broad programming communities often restrict self-promotion, and that subreddit rules vary. Direct access to the live Reddit rules pages was unavailable during this check, so no subreddit is marked as verified/approved for posting.
3. A bare link to a personal story is a weaker fit for a project-showcase community than a post that contributes a specific lesson, a concrete build, and an honest request for feedback.
4. The existing project record identifies the article as the first external publication. The article URL is recorded in `docs/story-log.md`: https://gitback2life.hashnode.dev/i-started-building-again-here-s-what-actually-happened

## Evaluation
- **Best low-risk first step:** Share the article individually with a small number of people who already have a genuine interest in the project, using a short note that explains what the article covers and makes no request for support or engagement.
- **Platform-native step:** Participate normally on Hashnode—read relevant posts, leave useful comments, and respond to genuine reactions to the article. Do not manufacture engagement.
- **Community posting:** Defer public Reddit promotion until the exact target community's live rules and recent norms are checked. If a future post is appropriate, lead with a concrete lesson from the build and disclose that it is the author's project; do not cross-post identical promotional copy.
- **Measurement:** Record only observable outcomes (for example, a reply, a useful question, or a concrete suggestion). Do not infer success or failure from silence alone.

## Recommendation
Run one small, human-scale distribution experiment: a few direct shares to genuinely interested readers plus normal participation on Hashnode. Do not create new accounts solely for promotion and do not post to Reddit based on third-party rule summaries alone.

## Uncertainties
- Live Reddit rules could not be fetched directly in this check.
- No external sharing or community post has been performed as part of this research.
- There is not yet enough response data to judge whether the article's framing or distribution is effective.

## Decision
Research and recommendation only. No new distribution platform or posting venue has been adopted by the operator.
