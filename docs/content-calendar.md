# gitback2life — Starter Content Calendar

## Weekly rhythm

### Update 1 — Build
What was built, changed, or fixed.

### Update 2 — Learn
One concrete concept learned and how it was applied.

### Update 3 — Progress
A factual update on the rebuilding process or a milestone.

## Monthly rhythm

### Monthly review
- Projects completed
- Skills learned
- Problems encountered
- Opportunities tested
- What worked
- What failed
- Next month's objective

## Content rule
Prefer concrete evidence and lessons over generic motivational posts.
