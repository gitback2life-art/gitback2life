This file is public. Record only information that is intentionally safe to publish.

Do not include:
- exact income, balances, debts, benefit amounts, or other sensitive financial details;
- private medical or legal information;
- account identifiers;
- private addresses or contact information;
- details that could reasonably identify the person behind the project.

Use general descriptions or ranges when they are sufficient to document progress.
