# gitback2life — Scam & Safety Checklist

Reject or stop to investigate anything that asks the project to:

- pay money to unlock earnings
- buy cryptocurrency
- receive and forward money
- share passwords or account access
- install remote-control software at another person's direction
- purchase equipment from a required supplier
- pay a hiring or training fee before work begins
- falsify information
- bypass a platform's rules
- send government ID without a legitimate verified reason
- act under artificial time pressure

## Support safety
Never publish a gift-card PIN, one-time claim link, password, or recovery code. Verify support instructions against official provider documentation or trusted platform rules.
