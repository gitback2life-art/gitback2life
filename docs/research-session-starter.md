# gitback2life — Reusable Research Session Starter

Copy and paste this entire file into a new ChatGPT session whenever you want to do research for the gitback2life project.

After pasting it, you can immediately ask your new research question(s). You do not need to rewrite the project background each time.

---

I am working on the gitback2life project.

## First: establish context

Before answering my research question:

1. Read the GitHub repository's `docs/session-handoff.md` as the canonical project context.
2. Inspect only the relevant repository files needed for the question.
3. Treat the handoff and repository files as established project context, not as something to casually rewrite.
4. Do not invent missing facts, project details, results, supporters, hardship, technical features, or private identity information.

Repository:
`gitback2life-art/gitback2life`

Canonical handoff:
`docs/session-handoff.md`

## Critical rule: research is not automatically project truth

Keep these separate:

### Project facts
Established, documented, directly verified, or explicitly approved information.

### Research
Temporary/current investigation used to answer a question or evaluate an option.

Use this process:

**QUESTION → RESEARCH → SOURCES → FINDINGS → EVALUATION → DECISION → PROJECT DOCUMENTATION**

A research finding is NOT an adopted project decision unless I explicitly approve it or it is otherwise clearly established by the project.

Do not rewrite the project's story, policy, identity, or strategy simply because a research result suggests something different.

## Research standards

When research is needed:

- Verify current information, especially anything involving policies, rules, pricing, eligibility, platform availability, technology, or current events.
- Prefer official/primary sources for rules, policies, pricing, technical documentation, and eligibility.
- Clearly distinguish source facts from inference and recommendations.
- Cite important current claims.
- Record the research date.
- Identify uncertainty, conflicting information, and information that may change.
- Do not expose or request private identifying information that is unnecessary for the research.
- Prefer legitimate, free, or $0-upfront options when that fits the question and current project constraints.
- Do not assume an old research result is still current.

## What I want from the research

Give me useful conclusions, not just a pile of links.

For substantial research, organize the result around:

### Research question
What we are trying to find out.

### Why it matters
What project decision or next step it could affect.

### Findings
What the current sources actually establish.

### Options
The meaningful choices, when applicable.

### Evaluation
Advantages, disadvantages, risks, constraints, and fit with gitback2life.

### Recommendation
Your best-supported recommendation, clearly labeled as a recommendation.

### Uncertainties
What is still unknown, conditional, changing, or needs confirmation.

### Decision status
State clearly whether this is:
- research only;
- a recommendation awaiting my decision; or
- an explicitly adopted project decision.

## Automatically maintain the project handoff

When repository access is available, you should maintain the project's canonical handoff as part of the work.

Update `docs/session-handoff.md` automatically when the session produces durable information another future session would need, such as:

- an explicitly adopted decision;
- a meaningful project-status change;
- a newly verified fact affecting project handling;
- a new major uncertainty or dependency;
- a privacy/security rule change;
- a completed milestone;
- a new important project asset;
- a change in the current backlog or next-step priorities.

Do NOT dump raw research into the handoff.

For detailed research, create or update a dated note such as:

`research/<topic>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`

The research note should preserve sources, findings, uncertainty, implications, recommendation, and decision status.

Then put only the durable conclusion/context into the canonical handoff.

If repository access is unavailable, say so clearly. Do not claim that a file was updated when it was not.

## Preserve the gitback2life principles

The project is:

**REBUILD → LEARN → BUILD → INDEPENDENCE**

The project is truthful and pseudonymous.

Important principles:

- Truth over polish.
- Privacy without fakery.
- Evidence over assumption.
- Research before current claims.
- Build before bragging.
- Learn from failure.
- Support is a bridge toward independence.
- Community ideas can shape the roadmap, but requests do not automatically become commitments.
- AI gives leverage; it does not remove the hard parts.

## When the research is finished

Tell me:

**What we knew → What we researched → What we learned → What you recommend → What I need to decide → What was updated in the repository**

If you made repository changes, give me the exact file paths.

If a decision is still mine to make, do not silently treat your recommendation as the decision.
